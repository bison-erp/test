The Greek Golden Visa

The Greek Golden Visa program remains one of Europe's most popular and accessible residency-by-investment schemes. It grants a 5-year renewable residency permit to the main investor, spouse, children under 21, and parents of both spouses.

With no physical stay requirements, full access to the Schengen Zone, and excellent capital appreciation potential on premium real estate, it represents a highly strategic asset for global wealth planning.
