### Belair and Merl

Belair is the capital's established family district: townhouses, villas and large apartments a few minutes from the centre. Merl extends it around the park, with a quieter fabric of houses.

### Limpertsberg

An elegant district north of the old town, valued for its townhouses, schools and the Glacis. Good family homes are in demand and tend to sell quickly.

### Ville-Haute

The UNESCO-listed historic centre offers rare apartments in period buildings, as well as prestige offices around Boulevard Royal.

### Kirchberg

Home to the European institutions and many banks, the plateau mixes offices, recent residences and new developments. The sources we consulted publish no reliable average for the district alone, so prices are best assessed scheme by scheme.

### Strassen and Bertrange

These communes on the western edge of the city offer more houses with gardens, quick access to the centre and proximity to the European School in Mamer. Prices generally remain high, at the upper end of the canton's range.

## How buying works: preliminary contract, notary and duties

As a rule, Luxembourg places no restriction on foreign buyers. A typical purchase:

1. **Written offer**, then a **compromis de vente** (preliminary sale agreement), which binds both parties. Include conditions precedent, especially for mortgage approval.
2. **Notarial deed**: the notary checks title, collects the duties and registers the sale.
3. **Duties**: according to guichet.lu, about 7% of the price (6% registration and 1% transcription), plus a 3% municipal surcharge on certain buildings in Luxembourg City, notably commercial or mixed-use ones. Notary fees, set by a statutory scale, come on top.
4. **New builds**: VAT is included in the price. The 3% rate applies only to a main residence, with the benefit capped at €50,000; a buy-to-let purchase bears the standard 17% rate.

The **Bëllegen Akt** tax credit, which reduces duties by up to €40,000 per buyer, is reserved for individuals who live in the property as their main residence within the legal deadlines. A non-resident buying a pied-à-terre or a rental property therefore pays full duties. ## Financing your purchase

Luxembourg banks lend to non-residents with a strong file (income, currency, assets). CSSF Regulation 20-08 caps loan-to-value ratios at up to 100% for first-time buyers of a main residence, 90% for other main residences and 80% for all other properties, buy-to-let included. In practice, plan for a substantial deposit and obtain an indicative bank offer before making an offer. We work alongside your bankers and tax advisers.

## Buying offices in Luxembourg

Luxembourg is also a sought-after office market for private investors and for companies that want to own their premises. According to Cushman & Wakefield, BNP Paribas Real Estate and JLL, prime rents at the end of 2025 stood at around €54–55/m² per month in the central business district, €42–43/m² in Kirchberg and €39–41/m² at Cloche d'Or. JLL put vacancy at around 4%, and BNP Paribas Real Estate reported a prime yield close to 4.6%. These figures describe the market; they are not a promise of returns.

### Can non-residents buy property in Luxembourg?
Yes, as a rule there is no restriction on foreign buyers. A purchase does not, however, give any right of residence.

### What are the buying costs in Luxembourg?
Around 7% in registration and transcription duties, a possible municipal surcharge on certain buildings in Luxembourg City, and notary fees. Ask the notary for a detailed statement before signing.

### Who qualifies for the Bëllegen Akt?
Individuals buying a home to live in themselves as their main residence, within the legal deadlines. Second homes and rental properties are excluded.

According to Immotop.lu, asking prices averaged around €11,300/m² in the capital in Q2 2026, and more in Belair. Exceptional homes are priced case by case.

Belair, Merl and Limpertsberg for neighbourhood life, Kirchberg for proximity to the institutions, and Strassen or Bertrange for houses with gardens. Schools and your workplace will also shape the choice.
