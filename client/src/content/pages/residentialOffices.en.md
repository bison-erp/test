**Luxury property investment in Europe** is about more than choosing a beautiful address: you have to weigh markets with very different prices, acquisition costs and liquidity, and then buy the right property at the right price. Acropolis Real Estate advises international buyers, families and private investors alike, on the acquisition of exceptional residences and prime offices, acting solely on their behalf.

## Set the strategy before searching

The first question is not "where?" but "what for?". A main home, a pied-à-terre used a few weeks a year, a family house to be passed on, or a let office building all call for different criteria. Every engagement therefore begins with a written brief:

- **Use**: personal occupation, holiday lets, long-term letting, company headquarters.
- **Horizon**: intended holding period and exit scenario.
- **Total budget**: price, acquisition costs, works, furnishing and annual holding costs.
- **Ownership structure**: in your own name, through a property company or a foreign vehicle; this is a matter for your notary and tax adviser, whom we involve from the outset.

This brief avoids the costliest mistake of all: buying an attractive property that does not serve your wealth objectives.

## Exceptional residences: what we check

Beyond location, a prime property is judged on details a listing never shows. For every shortlisted residence we examine:

- whether the price is consistent with recent comparable sales in the street or building;
- the condition of the building, surveys, and works approved or expected in the co-ownership;
- planning rules, easements and, on the coast, hazard zones;
- light, noise, the immediate surroundings and the quality of the common parts;
- potential for added value and ease of resale.

## Prime offices: a different discipline

Buying offices, whether to house your own company or to let, follows an investor's logic. We analyse the tenancy schedule (current leases, remaining fixed terms, tenant covenant strength), non-recoverable charges, technical and environmental compliance, and the depth of occupier demand in the area. In France, the commercial lease and its rent review rules deserve a careful reading; in Luxembourg, proximity to Kirchberg, Cloche d'Or or the station weighs heavily on demand.

For a business owner setting up a head office, we also factor in accessibility, the standing of the address and room to expand. In the Grand Duchy, we act as a [Luxembourg property finder](/real-estate-luxembourg "Luxembourg property finder for residents and non-residents") for residents and non-residents alike.

### What budget should I plan for a luxury property investment in Europe?
On top of the price, allow for acquisition costs (around 7–8% for older property in France, 7% in Luxembourg before any municipal surcharge, a transfer tax of around 3% on resale property in Greece), works, furnishing and annual charges. Your notary will calculate the exact amount.

### Is it better to buy a residence or offices?
It depends on the objective. A residence primarily serves personal use and succession planning; let offices aim at income and require an investor's analysis (leases, tenants, charges). We can handle both and help you decide.

### Can non-residents buy freely in France or Luxembourg?
As a rule, yes: neither France nor Luxembourg prohibits property purchases by non-residents. Banks will, however, ask for extensive evidence of the source of funds, and financing can be more demanding.

### Should I buy directly or through a company?
That depends on your country of tax residence, your family situation and how the property will be used. It is a question for a notary and a tax adviser; we bring them into the project at the briefing stage.

### Are rental returns guaranteed?
No. No return is guaranteed. We present prudent assumptions based on observed rents, voids and charges, and we flag the points that need attention.
